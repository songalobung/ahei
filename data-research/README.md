## What this pass found

The Wikidata Query Service returned 1,888 rows and 1,877 unique entities explicitly typed as a university and associated with a country on the African continent. After deduplication and filtering to current African country ISO codes, 1,860 candidates remain across 53 of 54 countries. Seychelles has zero candidates in this extract; this is a coverage gap, not evidence that no university exists there.

The discovery query used Wikidata's direct `instance of university` classification rather than the broader subclass hierarchy. This avoids many colleges and polytechnics but can omit legitimate universities classified using a more specific type. The harvest also returned historical country labels and entities outside the current 54-country scope; the raw extract is retained so exclusions can be audited.

## Important limitations

These rows are leads for research, **not confirmed institutions**:

- Wikidata entries can be stale, duplicated, incomplete, historical, incorrectly classified, or attached to the wrong country.
- An official-website candidate is not a verified working URL or proof of current recognition.
- A listed institution may be closed, merged, a campus, or not currently accredited.
- This pass did not collect verified degree-program catalogs, tuition, admissions rules, scholarships, accreditation decisions, or institution-level research metrics.
- The candidate count is not a total for AHEI and must not be presented as the complete number of African universities.

No missing facts should be inferred from the candidate list. Keep unknown values empty and record the reason/status separately.

## Field collection and validation rules

For each institution, the research phase should capture:

| Field group | Fields to establish | Preferred evidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Identity | Official name, aliases, institution type, active/closed/merged status | National regulator register, institution's official site |
| Recognition | Regulator, recognition/accreditation scope and status, decision date, expiry/review date | National regulator or accreditation authority |
| Location | Country, city, campus locations, coordinates | Official institution contact/location page; verify map coordinates |
| Contact | Official website, public email/phone where appropriate | Institution's official domain |
| Academics | Faculties, departments, programs, qualification level, mode, duration | Current official catalog/prospectus and regulator program register |
| Student costs | Tuition and mandatory fees, currency, academic year, student category | Current official fee schedule |
| Admissions | Entry requirements, application dates/deadlines, intake | Current official admissions page/prospectus |
| Scholarships | Provider, eligibility, award/coverage, dates, official application URL | Scholarship provider or institution |
| Research | Publication/citation counts and observation window, if used | Reusable bibliographic data with affiliations and documented method |
| Provenance | URL, publisher, access date, effective date, evidence excerpt/reference, reviewer, rights/terms | Required for every consequential claim |

Never treat an institution-level accreditation as proof that every program is accredited. Record the scope at which the regulator made the decision.

## Recommended research workflow before implementation

1. Discover the official ministry, regulator, and accreditation authority for each of the 54 countries.
2. Obtain a current official list of recognized universities and preserve the source's publication/update date and reuse terms.
3. Reconcile the official lists against this candidate file, explicitly recording additions, removals, duplicates, mergers, and unresolved cases.
4. Validate institution identity, operating status, official domain, and recognition before collecting deeper profile details.
5. Collect programs and student-decision data from institution or regulator publications; attach a source and effective date to every field.
6. Identify licensing and access restrictions before bulk collection or republication. Do not scrape in breach of terms, robots rules, or copyright.
7. Report field completeness and verification coverage by country. Leave unsupported values unknown.
8. Only then use the verified data sample to finalize the database schema, launch coverage, and ranking methodology.

This is an incremental research dataset, not the complete data requested for launch. The tracker has 54 country rows; only the country-level discovery pass is broad. Official directories have been captured or located for a subset, and institution identity reconciliation plus program-level, tuition, admissions, scholarship, accreditation, and research evidence remain open work across the continent.
