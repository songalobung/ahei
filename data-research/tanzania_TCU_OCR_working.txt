As of August 2026, the number of approved university institutions operating in Tanzania is 56, whereas 24 are public-owned and 32 are private-owned university institutions. The public is hereby notified that any university institution which does not appear in this list is NOT authorised to offer university education in Tanzania in line with the Universities Act, Cap. 346.
