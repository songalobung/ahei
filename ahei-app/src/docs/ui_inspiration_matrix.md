## 1. Visual Breakdown of Industry Leaders

### A. The Masthead & Brand Accent
- **QS TopUniversities:** Vibrant warm gold/amber `#F59E0B` banner with high-contrast bold modern sans typography. Crisp navigation bar with clear utility actions (*Apply with QS, Log in, Sign up*).
- **Times Higher Education (THE):** Deep navy header with iconic red/crimson accents, clean white cards with subtle borders (`#E5E7EB`), and multi-tabbed sub-headers (*Overview · Rankings · Jobs · Insights*).

### B. Rankings Table Architecture (The Gold Standard)
- **Compact Data Rows:** The table is NOT a bunch of huge floating cards. It is a dense, highly scannable table.
- **Visual Score Indicators:**
  - Notice the **radar / circular score wheels** or clean comparative percentage bars next to overall scores in THE (Image 1).
  - Explicit column breakdown for indicators: *Research Quality, Teaching, Industry, International Outlook*.
- **Quick Action Trays:** Each row has a quick "Compare" checkbox, an institutional crest/logo, the city and country with a small flag, and an expand toggle.

### C. Institutional Profile Page (Oxford & MIT Examples)
- **Top Brand Hero Banner:** Dark header containing:
  - University Crest / Logo square badge.
  - Institution Name in crisp bold typography.
  - Location breadcrumbs (*Oxford, United Kingdom* / *Cambridge, United States*).
  - Prominent buttons: *Visit Website*, *Save*, *Share*, *Add to Compare*.
- **Key Metric Ribbon (The 4 Stats):**
  - QS/THE uses 4 prominent stat blocks: *#1 World Rank*, *Overall Score 98.5*, *Student-to-Staff Ratio (10.5)*, *International Students % (44%)*.
- **Ranking Movement History Chart:**
  - An interactive multi-year trend line showing historical movement (2020 → 2026).
- **Tuition & Cost of Living Breakdown:**
  - Split into *Tuition Fees* vs. *Estimated Living Costs* (Accommodation, Food, Study Supplies).
- **Subject Directory Matrix:**
  - Clear grid of subject disciplines offered (*Arts & Humanities, Business & Economics, Computer Science, Engineering, Law, Life Sciences, Medical & Health, Physical Sciences*).

## 2. Immediate Implementation Strategy for AHEI

1. **Adopt the Premium Academic Color Palette:**
   - Deep Midnight Blue (`#0B132B` / `#1C2541`) for authoritative institution blocks.
   - Warm Amber Gold (`#F59E0B` / `#D97706`) as the signature ranking accent (inspired by QS).
   - Crisp Pure White surfaces (`#FFFFFF`) with subtle `#E2E8F0` boundaries and `#F8FAFC` backgrounds.
2. **Re-engineer the AHEI Rankings Table:**
   - Add institutional crest badges and country flags.
   - Include indicator score wheels/bars (*Teaching, Citations, Research, Governance*).
   - Sticky table headers with real-time column sorting and quick compare triggers.
3. **Rebuild the University Dossier Profile:**
   - Hero banner with university crest, key facts ribbon, and official links.
   - Dedicated Cost of Living & Tuition estimator.
   - Subject directory matrix.
   - Sourced provenance drawer with explicit verification badges.
