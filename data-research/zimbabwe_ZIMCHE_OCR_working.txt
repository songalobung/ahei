INTRODUCTION Zimbabwe has, as of June 2024, twenty Higher Education Institutions registered with the Zimbabwe Council for Higher Education. There are thirteen state universities and seven private institutions that offer degrees. This booklet serves to inform the public that the institutions cited therein are deemed duly registered with the Zimbabwe Council for Higher Education. The cited programmes offered by the institutions are accredited by ZIMCHE. The information shown in this booklet covers:- • • name of institution; mandate of the institution; when the institution was established; contact details of the institution; location of the institution; programmes (undergraduate and postgraduate) offered by the institution; and affiliate colleges of the institution and the programmes offered at the affiliate. 2
